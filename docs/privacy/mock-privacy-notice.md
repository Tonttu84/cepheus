## 2. What we collect (and what we never store)

### What we collect
- **Twice-weekly check-in taps:** Two numbers (0 to 4) rating your mood and interest.
- **Fortnightly depression total:** A total score (0 to 27) from the standard PHQ-9 questions.
- **Wearable sleep and movement summary:** Daily step counts, sleep duration, and bedtime regularity from your ring or watch.
- **Optional camera check:** If you turn on the camera check, your device measures facial movements for 60 seconds. Only derived trend numbers are sent.
- **Crisis disclosures:** If you answer that you have had thoughts of self-harm, a crisis notice is created so clinic staff can offer immediate help.

### What we NEVER store
- **No video or photos:** The camera check processes images directly on your phone or computer. Video frames never leave your device.
- **No audio or voice recordings:** Spoken check-ins are turned into numbers on your device. Sound recordings are never sent.
- **No individual question answers:** While answering a questionnaire, answers are kept in temporary server memory. Once complete, individual answers are wiped and only the total score is kept.
- **No typed words or free text:** There are no open text boxes. This prevents accidental sharing of sensitive private notes.
- **No phone storage:** Nothing is stored in your phone's browser storage. If you lose your phone, it holds no check-in data.

---

## 3. Why we collect it

We use this information for one purpose: to help your clinic notice if your wellbeing begins to slip after treatment, so a nurse can call you and discuss whether you need a booster session or extra support.

The app never makes a diagnosis and never decides your care. It only flags that a nurse should call you.

---

## 4. Who sees your information?

- **You:** You see your scheduled rounds, your suggestions, and whether you are staying near your baseline.
- **Clinic nurses and doctors:** When answers stay low for two rounds in a row, clinic staff see a flag. The flag names only the broad area that changed (such as "sleep" or "check-in") and the date. Staff never see raw camera readings, raw sensor graphs, or individual question answers.
- **Emergency help:** If you report self-harm, the app shows direct emergency contact options (112 and crisis lines) immediately on your screen.

We never sell your data, use it for advertising, or send it outside the European Union.

---

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep your check-in numbers during your post-treatment follow-up period (usually 8 to 12 weeks). After your follow-up ends, your check-in records are closed and removed according to the clinic's medical archive schedule.

---

## 6. Your rights and how to stop

Under data protection law, you have the right to:

1. **See your data:** You can view your check-in history in the app at any time.
2. **Turn off any sensor:** You can turn the camera check or wearable sync off at any moment in your settings.
3. **Stop taking part:** You can leave the check-in program at any time. Stopping never affects your regular healthcare or your relationship with your clinic.
4. **Ask for deletion:** You can ask your clinic to delete your check-in records.
5. **Ask for a copy:** You can request a machine-readable copy of your check-in totals.

To use any of these rights, contact your clinic's Data Protection Officer at `[dpo@clinic-domain.example]`.
