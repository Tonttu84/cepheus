# How the Build Meets Data Protection Requirements

> **Draft for a hackathon demonstration. Written by an AI agent, not reviewed by a lawyer, not legal advice.**

This document provides a line-by-line compliance analysis of the Cepheus post-TMS wellbeing check-in system against key European and Finnish healthcare data protection principles (GDPR Regulation (EU) 2016/679, Tietosuojalaki 1050/2018, and Laki sosiaali- ja terveydenhuollon asiakastietojen käsittelystä 703/2023).

To maintain rigorous transparency, this analysis categorises every safeguard into three distinct states:
1. **Designed in:** Implemented in the application code today, verified by automated unit, integration, or structural tests.
2. **Required of a deployment:** Architectural and governance requirements that a real clinical installation must provide, documented in detail in [`production-requirements.md`](production-requirements.md).
3. **Not done:** Deliberate prototype simplifications, simulations, or future integrations not implemented in this demonstration.

## 2. Distinction: Designed In vs Required of a Deployment vs Not Done

To prevent confusion between hackathon demonstration capabilities and production readiness, the table below highlights the operational boundaries across each functional domain:

### Summary of Differences

1. **Authentication & Identification:**
   - **Designed in:** Interface abstraction for strong electronic identification (`src/server/auth/eid-provider.ts`, decision D34); role-based session authorization in domain logic (`src/domain/access/decide.ts`).
   - **Required of a deployment:** Integration with certified Finnish Suomi.fi e-Identification broker (via bank ID or mobile certificate) and named, MFA-secured staff accounts ([Requirements §4](production-requirements.md#4-strong-patient-identification-and-named-audited-staff-accounts)).
   - **Not done:** Direct live connection to Suomi.fi e-Identification APIs; hardware security keys (FIDO2 / WebAuthn) for clinical personnel.

2. **Hosting Infrastructure & Data Boundaries:**
   - **Designed in:** Zero external transfers in core clinical flows; off-by-default EU-hosted confidential compute model for optional nurse brief formatting (`src/server/verda/client.ts`, decision D44); database isolation boundary (`src/__tests__/storage-boundary.test.ts`).
   - **Required of a deployment:** Hosting the web service and PostgreSQL database on certified EU/EEA infrastructure (such as UpCloud Helsinki or Verda GPU nodes holding ISO 27001 certifications); zero transfer of personal data outside the EU/EEA ([Requirements §1 & §2](production-requirements.md#1-hosting-and-healthcare-infrastructure)).
   - **Not done:** Provisioned production cloud cluster; database volume encryption at rest (TDE) on PostgreSQL.

3. **Data Protection Governance & Contracts:**
   - **Designed in:** Data inventory (`docs/privacy/data-inventory.md`); immutable access audit ledger (`AccessLog` in `prisma/schema.prisma`); purpose limitation enforcement (`src/usecases/__tests__/purpose-consistency.test.ts`).
   - **Required of a deployment:** Executed GDPR Article 28 Data Processing Agreements (DPAs); signed Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) approved by a designated Data Protection Officer (DPO); operational 72-hour breach reporting procedure ([Requirements §5](production-requirements.md#5-processor-agreements-impact-assessment-named-governance-breach-procedures-and-system-enforced-retention)).
   - **Not done:** Formal regulatory filings, institutional review board approvals, or live supervisory notifications.

4. **Data Retention & Lifecycle Management:**
   - **Designed in:** Volatile server memory for in-progress questionnaire items (decision D45); expiring one-time enrolment codes; cascading database deletions on participant removal.
   - **Required of a deployment:** Automated system purge workers enforcing defined post-treatment cohort retention and medical archive balancing ([Requirements §5](production-requirements.md#5-processor-agreements-impact-assessment-named-governance-breach-procedures-and-system-enforced-retention)).
   - **Not done:** Automated scheduled cron purge workers (prototype relies on manual database resets).

5. **Environment Separation & Patient Data:**
   - **Designed in:** Test fixtures and simulated participant seed generator (`prisma/seed.ts`); visual simulated data badges across all screens (`src/components/simulated-badge.tsx`).
   - **Required of a deployment:** Absolute prohibition of real patient data in non-production environments; isolated development and staging clusters ([Requirements §6](production-requirements.md#6-real-patient-data-strictly-prohibited-in-non-production-environments)).
   - **Not done:** Production patient database ingestion (the demonstration uses synthetic cohorts exclusively).

## 3. Finnish and EU Regulatory Landscape (Beyond GDPR)

A real healthcare service operating this product in Finland must verify compliance with several additional statutes beyond the GDPR:

1. **Finnish Data Protection Act (Tietosuojalaki 1050/2018):**  
   Specifies national exceptions and powers of the Data Protection Ombudsman, including processing of special categories of personal data in healthcare under Section 6. *(To be checked by a professional.)*
2. **Client and Patient Data Act (Laki sosiaali- ja terveydenhuollon asiakastietojen käsittelystä 703/2023):**  
   Governs recording, retention, information security classification (A- and B-class systems), and Kanta integration requirements for all information systems handling patient records in Finland. *(To be checked by a professional.)*
3. **Act on the Secondary Use of Health and Social Data (Laki sosiaali- ja terveystietojen toissijaisesta käytöstä 552/2019):**  
   Regulates using aggregated or pseudonymous health data for research, statistics, or algorithmic model development through the data permit authority Findata. *(To be checked by a professional.)*
4. **EU Medical Device Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2017/745, MDR) & MDCG 2019-11 Rev. 1:**  
   Software providing clinical decision support, warning flags, or relapse risk assessment for healthcare professionals qualifies as Medical Device Software (MDSW) under Annex VIII Rule 11 (typically Class IIa), requiring conformity assessment by a Notified Body before clinical deployment. *(To be checked by a professional.)*
